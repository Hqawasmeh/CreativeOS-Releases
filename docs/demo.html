Qanteak OS brings work and business operations into one connected workspace.

Qanteak OS is a Windows desktop work operating system for managing projects, tasks, clients, documents, files, reviews, finance, automations, databases, and AI-assisted workflows from one place.

Download current Windows build View pricing

Short product walkthrough

A typical Qanteak workflow connects the operational pieces that are usually split across separate tools.

1

Plan the work

Create projects, tasks, deadlines, calendars, timelines and goals.

2

Run the client work

Keep client records, files, documents, reviews, approvals and project history connected.

3

Manage the business

Track invoices, estimates, expenses, revenue and project profitability alongside delivery.

4

Automate and assist

Use automations, databases, Context Spaces and Qanteak AI to reduce repetitive admin work.

Core product areas

Qanteak is designed as one connected operating layer rather than a collection of unrelated mini-apps.

Projects & Tasks

Projects, assignments, due dates, boards, calendars, timelines, workload and progress tracking.

Clients & CRM

Client profiles, contacts, leads, notes, project history, quotes and related financial activity.

Documents & Files

Knowledge, project documents, file organization, versioning, delivery and linked workspace context.

Business & Finance

Invoices, estimates, expenses, payments, revenue summaries and profitability foundations.

Workspace & Databases

Typed properties, relations, rollups, formulas, multiple database views, forms and dashboards.

Automations & AI

Trigger-condition-action workflows, approvals, Context Spaces, Skills, agent planning and specialist intelligence.

Digital product fulfillment

Qanteak OS is a digital software product. The standard product is intended to be fulfilled electronically without customized professional services or manual project delivery. Customers receive access to the software and subscription entitlement digitally after checkout. Product support may be provided separately when needed.
