Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Qanteak OS is designed to help creative professionals manage projects, clients, tasks, files, reviews, invoices and related business workflows. This policy explains the main categories of information Qanteak OS may process when you use the software and website.

Information we may process

  • Account information such as email address, display name and authentication identifiers.
  • Subscription and entitlement information required to determine whether your account can access Qanteak OS.
  • Workspace information you choose to store or synchronize, such as projects, clients, tasks, notes, invoice metadata and review metadata.
  • Technical information such as application version, platform, diagnostic events and crash information when those features are enabled.

Local files

Qanteak OS may index files and folders that you explicitly allow the desktop application to access. Local file access is used for features such as search, project linking, version tracking, previews and opening files in Windows. Qanteak OS does not claim ownership of your files.

Cloud and connected services

Account authentication and workspace synchronization may use Supabase. Optional integrations may connect to services such as Google Drive, Calendar and Gmail when you authorize them. Connected services receive only the requests needed for features you choose to use and remain subject to their own privacy terms.

Payments

Qanteak OS is being integrated with Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record for paid subscriptions. Payment card information is handled by the payment provider rather than stored directly by Qanteak OS. Qanteak may receive limited order, customer and subscription metadata needed to manage access to the software.

AI features

When cloud AI features are enabled, prompts and the workspace context required to answer them may be sent to the configured AI service. Qanteak should not send unrelated local file contents automatically; users should avoid placing sensitive information in AI prompts unless they intend it to be processed by that service.

Analytics and diagnostics

Qanteak may process product-usage events and crash diagnostics when those features are enabled. These are intended for reliability, security and product improvement rather than advertising profiling.

Security

We use account authentication, backend authorization and row-level access controls intended to restrict user data to authorized accounts. No software or online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Account and workspace information may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, billing, legal or operational purposes. Local backups and recovery snapshots remain on your device until removed or replaced according to the application's retention behavior.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Qanteak OS develops or its service providers change. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
