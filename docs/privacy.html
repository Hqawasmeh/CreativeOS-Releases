Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Qanteak OS is designed to help creative professionals manage projects, clients, tasks, files, reviews, invoices and related business workflows. This policy explains the categories of information Qanteak OS may process when you use the software and website.

Information we may process

  • Account information such as email address, display name and authentication identifiers.
  • Subscription and entitlement information required to determine whether your account can access Qanteak OS.
  • Workspace information you choose to store or synchronize, such as projects, clients, tasks, notes, invoices and review metadata.
  • Technical information such as application version, platform, diagnostic events and crash information when enabled.

Local files

Qanteak OS may index files and folders that you explicitly allow the application to access. File access is used to provide desktop workflow features. Qanteak OS does not claim ownership of your files.

Cloud and connected services

Some features may use third-party infrastructure or connected services for authentication, synchronization, payments, email, calendar, cloud storage, analytics or AI processing. Those services may process information according to their own privacy terms.

Payments

Payment card details are intended to be handled by the selected payment provider or merchant-of-record platform rather than stored directly by Qanteak OS.

Security

We use account authentication, access controls and backend authorization intended to restrict user data to authorized accounts. No software or online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Account and workspace information may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, billing, legal or operational purposes. Data export and deletion tooling may evolve as the product moves from pre-release to general availability.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Qanteak OS develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
