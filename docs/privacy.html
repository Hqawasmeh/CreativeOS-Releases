Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

CreativeOS is designed to help creative professionals manage projects, clients, tasks, files, reviews, invoices and related business workflows. This policy explains the categories of information CreativeOS may process when you use the software.

Information we may process

  • Account information such as email address, display name and authentication identifiers.
  • Subscription and entitlement information required to determine whether your account can access CreativeOS.
  • Workspace information you choose to store or synchronize, such as projects, clients, tasks, notes, invoices and review metadata.
  • Technical information such as application version, platform, error reports and diagnostic events when enabled.

Local files

CreativeOS may index files and folders that you explicitly allow the application to access. File access is used to provide desktop workflow features. CreativeOS does not claim ownership of your files.

Cloud services

Some CreativeOS features may use third-party infrastructure or services, including authentication, cloud synchronization, payments, email, calendar, file storage, analytics or AI providers. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy terms.

Payments

Payment card details are intended to be handled by the selected payment provider or merchant-of-record platform rather than stored directly by CreativeOS.

Security

CreativeOS uses account authentication and access controls intended to restrict user data to authorized accounts. No software or online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Account and workspace information may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, legal, billing or operational purposes. Account deletion and data export capabilities may be expanded as the product moves from pre-release to general availability.

Changes

This policy may be updated as CreativeOS develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
