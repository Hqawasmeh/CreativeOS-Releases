THE IDEA BEHIND QANTEAK

Good work deserves
a better home.

Projects in one app. Clients in another. Feedback in a message thread. The work connects—but the tools often do not.

It starts with
the connections.

Qanteak is built around a simple idea: the project, the client, the brief, the files and the invoice should understand that they belong to the same work.

That shared context makes everyday work easier to follow. It also creates a more useful foundation for automation and AI, because the important relationships are already there.

Our ambition is to build an operating system for your work. A flexible space to plan, deliver, learn and run a business with a clearer view of the whole.
