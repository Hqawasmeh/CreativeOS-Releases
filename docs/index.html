What Qanteak OS does

A working system around your creative tools.

Qanteak OS does not replace Photoshop, Illustrator or DaVinci Resolve. It organizes everything around them: the client, the brief, the deadline, the files, the feedback, the invoice and the next action.

Keep every part of the job in context.

Instead of opening five apps just to understand one project, Qanteak keeps the moving pieces connected. Open a project and see the client, current tasks, linked files, reviews, documents, invoices and AI context in one place.

The problem

The more successful your creative work becomes, the harder it gets to keep everything under control.

More clients means more folders. More projects means more versions. More feedback means more messages. More tools means more places to check. Growth is good — but without a system around the work, the complexity grows even faster.

01

Your work disappears into messy files and folders.

Final-final-v4, duplicate exports, references, drafts and delivery files spread across drives until finding the right version becomes a task by itself.

02

Every client creates another layer of admin.

Briefs, deadlines, approvals, notes, invoices and conversations live in different places, so you keep rebuilding the project context from memory.

03

Your tools multiply faster than your workflow improves.

One app for tasks, another for files, another for feedback, another for billing — and you spend more time managing the system than doing the creative work.

The problem is not that you have too much work. The problem is that your work has no single operating layer.
The solution

One workspace that keeps the creative work, the client, the files and the business connected.

Qanteak OS turns scattered tools into one continuous operating layer around the project — so context stays with the work from the first brief to final delivery.

One connected workspace

Context that follows the project

Open a project and the important information is already there — what changed, what is waiting and what should happen next.

Creative + business in one flow

Move from production to feedback, delivery and billing without disconnecting the business side from the creative work.
