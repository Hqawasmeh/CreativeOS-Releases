GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Is the Free plan a trial?+

No. The Free plan is intended to remain available without a time limit, with lower usage and feature limits than the paid plans.

How does annual pricing work?+

Basic is $96 per year, Pro is $192 per year, and Teams is $180 per seat per year. The monthly equivalents shown for annual billing are charged as yearly totals. Sandbox payments are simulated.

Is Qanteak a browser app or desktop app?+

Qanteak is currently available as a Windows desktop release. This website lets you explore the product, create an account and download the app. macOS and mobile downloads are not available yet.

Does every plan include Qanteak AI?+

AI access varies by plan. The Free plan is limited, Basic includes expanded access, and Pro and Teams are designed for the advanced intelligence layer. Hosted model usage and live integrations remain dependent on configured services and availability.
