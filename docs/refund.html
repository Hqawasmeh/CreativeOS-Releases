Refund Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Qanteak OS is intended to be sold as subscription software. Paid checkout is being integrated with Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record, so payment processing, taxes and certain refund or dispute procedures may be handled through that platform.

Refund requests

Please contact support if you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged more than once, or cannot access paid Qanteak features because of a confirmed account or technical issue. Refund eligibility is assessed according to applicable consumer law, the circumstances of the purchase, and any terms displayed by the payment provider at checkout.

Cancellation

You can cancel a recurring subscription to prevent future renewal charges. Cancellation does not automatically mean an immediate refund of the current paid period. Where the payment provider keeps a cancelled subscription active through the end of the current billing period, Qanteak access may continue until that effective end date.

Pre-release access

Release-candidate or beta versions may contain bugs or incomplete features. If paid pre-release access is offered, any additional terms shown at checkout form part of the purchase conditions.

Payment disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact support before initiating a chargeback so the issue can be reviewed. This does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

How to request help

Send your account email, order or invoice reference if available, charge date and a brief explanation to hamza.workjo@gmail.com. Do not send full card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.
